# Presentations: content to editable artifacts

## Inputs

Topic, audience, purpose, duration, authorized materials, brand rules, output format and actual tools.

## Method

1. Specify audience, communication purpose, duration and actual materials; give every slide one clear reader job and intended understanding or decision.
2. Choose generation from materials, screenshot reconstruction, native-object-preserving edits or template-based work separately; their input contracts differ and reconstruction must not silently overwrite the source.
3. Separate locked brand fonts, colors, marks and terms from flexible layout; claims, data and citations require actual provenance.
4. Plan slides, canonical content/charts, notes and output locations; assign ownership of content, assets, native objects and export rather than creating competing sources.
5. Use actually available authorized local tooling for editable output, render every slide and inspect crop, typography, alignment, contrast, labels and evidence; PNG checks still require opening the PPTX.
6. Repair faults at the owning layer and resume from the last correct stage; deliver artifacts, sources, actual open checks and gaps, without calling a plan a generated presentation.

## Outputs and acceptance

Slide plan, editable presentation, speaker notes, preview and source/check ledger. Source data, charts and prose must agree; report passes only after actual rendering and opening.

## Dependency and licensing boundary

This is a bilingual MIT method adaptation, not a complete PPT Master installation. It bundles no scripts, icons, sounds, templates, brand images, PDF/EPUB conversion or model interfaces; complete external installation needs a separate check.

PyMuPDF AGPL/commercial licensing, EbookLib AGPL, CHUNK CC BY4 and icon/shape terms are not overridden by root MIT. These assets/dependencies are excluded. Platform noncommercial terms do not replace this package MIT rights.
