# Research Narrative and Paper Framework

This is a local adaptation of pinned upstream methods. Follow the current goal, approved plan, and file scope; put actual materials/results in the author-selected workspace, not built-in folders.

## Inputs

Research narrative report, actual results, citation evidence, author-selected venue, and approved writing scope.

## Workflow

1. Plan the manuscript around one main contribution, paragraph questions, figure purpose, and agreed acceptance; do not inherit the original fixed ICLR target.
2. Plot actual data and verify schematic relations; use editable local diagrams when an image service is absent without claiming the upstream generator ran.
3. Draft from the outline with provenance, separating methods/results and unconfirmed statements; obtain authorized current official templates without unreviewed conference assets.
4. Actually compile/open previews and check numerical consistency, pages, and citations; explicitly record proof checks and independently unverified theory.
5. Create claim/citation/integrity checks and bounded revision rounds; do not fabricate passes for unavailable upstream audit tools.

## Outputs

paper-plan.md, manuscript/actual preview, claim-audit.md, citation-audit.md, and handoff.md.

## Acceptance

- Acceptance and file scope are inspectable; figures/citations have actual provenance.
- Compilation, scientific review, and author approval have separate recorded statuses.

## Continue When

After material checks, hand off to the author/authorized independent reviewer before submission preparation.

- **LaTeX/PDF工具**: Needed only for the chosen output format; verify the actual compiler.

## Writing evidence and citations

1. Define section/figure purposes, main claims, actual evidence and acceptance before drafting.
2. Trace every numerical/scope statement to raw file/key/denominator; verify citation identity separately from full-text support.
3. Changed manuscript/evidence makes earlier audits specific to prior inputs; recheck affected content and repeated theorem/notation/scope statements.
