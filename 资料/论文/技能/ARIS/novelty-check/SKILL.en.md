## Inputs

The concrete method, assumptions and claimed novelty, known closest work, research scope and contribution dimensions.

## Method

1. Decompose novelty into mechanism, objective, theory, task setting or evaluation. Identify the actual contribution rather than comparing names and packaging.
2. Search mechanism synonyms, key components and citation chains of closest work. Record queries, dates, candidate identities and coverage gaps.
3. Verify that papers exist and read relevant method/result locations. Compare similarities, key differences, additional assumptions and whether the idea is merely a combination.
4. Distinguish source-supported differences from inference and unresolved points and propose minimum experiments or theoretical questions that discriminate existing explanations.
5. If a real independent reviewer is available within scope, provide the same method and raw evidence. Otherwise deliver a local verification draft without claiming cross-model novelty confirmation.

## Outputs and acceptance

A closest-work comparison, evidence locations, overlaps, defensible differences, search limits and validation questions. Novelty must not rely on omitted literature, invented citations or renamed mechanisms.
