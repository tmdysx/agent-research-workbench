# Prior-art search and scope record

This is a method adaptation, not a word-for-word translation. The complete original is retained separately. Use the current authorized workspace, goal, plan, applicable rules and latest handoff.

## Inputs

Invention features, synonyms, jurisdiction and time boundary.

## Method

1. Extract search concepts and multilingual terms from the problem, structure/steps and use.
2. Search patent and scholarly sources, recording queries, dates, classifications and document identities.
3. Compare elements, disclosure dates and relevance, retaining nearby approaches and counterexamples.
4. Freedom-to-operate discussion is preliminary risk discovery only; no hit does not prove absence or commercial clearance.

## Outputs and acceptance

Reproducible search log, prior-art table, element differences and scope limits.

Every conclusion/completion traces to actual files, sources and checks. Report missing inputs, missing independence and unperformed checks; creating an artifact does not replace platform delivery acceptance.
