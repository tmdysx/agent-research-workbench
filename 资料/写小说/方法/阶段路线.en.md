# Novel Writing · Stage Route

For each stage check inputs, deliver concrete outputs, and apply acceptance before continuing. Resume from actual completed records and expose gaps. Detailed skills below are options, not a mandatory sequence of every method.

## Brief and Reader

- **Inputs**：Author idea, genre/audience, length/pacing, and boundaries.
- **Outputs**：Creative brief, central conflict, promise, and undecided author choices.
- **Acceptance**：Respect the original request; proposals are not approved canon.
- **Continue when**：The author selects a direction for world/character design.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release)

## World and Characters

- **Inputs**：Creative brief, confirmed canon, characters, and constraints.
- **Outputs**：Canon ledger, character goals/relations, facts, and pending decisions.
- **Acceptance**：Motivations/rules are reviewable; new canon does not silently overwrite old canon.
- **Continue when**：The central conflict has characters capable of carrying the outline.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Novel Drafting and Single-Book State [biz:novel:nkrgxz:novel-writer] (optional method; not bundled in this release)

## Layered Outline

- **Inputs**：Canon/characters, story objective, length, and current progress.
- **Outputs**：Overall/volume/chapter goals, causal scenes, and foreshadowing ledger.
- **Acceptance**：Conflict develops causally and character knowledge/timeline stay consistent.
- **Continue when**：The current chapter goal is clear and the author agrees to this drafting scope.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Novel Drafting and Single-Book State [biz:novel:nkrgxz:novel-writer] (optional method; not bundled in this release)

## Draft and State

- **Inputs**：Current chapter goal, prior text, canon, and desired voice.
- **Outputs**：Versioned chapter, summary, character state, and new-fact ledger.
- **Acceptance**：Actual prose exists without viewpoint/time/knowledge conflicts; proposals are labeled.
- **Continue when**：The chapter is readable enough for author/review input.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Novel Drafting and Single-Book State [biz:novel:nkrgxz:novel-writer] (optional method; not bundled in this release)

## Continuity and Cold Read

- **Inputs**：Draft, bounded canon packet, and author review goals.
- **Outputs**：Issues with locations, severity, and revision proposals.
- **Acceptance**：Every issue traces to text; separate reader experience from factual inconsistency.
- **Continue when**：The author selects changes before revision.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Novel Drafting and Single-Book State [biz:novel:nkrgxz:novel-writer] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Independent Cold-Read Review [biz:novel:nkrgxz:zero-inheritance-review] (optional method; not bundled in this release)

## Author-Approved Revision

- **Inputs**：Author choices, issue list, and current text version.
- **Outputs**：New version preserving the original, differences, and canon changes.
- **Acceptance**：Changes follow the chosen scope, without unrequested voice/storyline changes.
- **Continue when**：Recheck passes before the next chapter or handoff.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Independent Cold-Read Review [biz:novel:nkrgxz:zero-inheritance-review] (optional method; not bundled in this release)

## Handoff and Manuscript

- **Inputs**：Accepted version, canon changes, progress, and open issues.
- **Outputs**：Current position, next task, versions, and author decisions pending.
- **Acceptance**：A new agent finds actual prose/canon without guessing from conversation.
- **Continue when**：The author specifies the next task/scope; assembling a manuscript does not publish it.

Optional methods: Stage-Based Novel Writing [biz:novel:xinganliu:web-novel-writing] (optional method; not bundled in this release) · Novel Drafting and Single-Book State [biz:novel:nkrgxz:novel-writer] (optional method; not bundled in this release)
