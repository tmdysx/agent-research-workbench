# Paper Writing · Stage Route

For each stage check inputs, deliver concrete outputs, and apply acceptance before continuing. Resume from actual completed records and expose gaps. Detailed skills below are options, not a mandatory sequence of every method.

## Topic and Hypothesis

- **Inputs**：Interests, observations, authorized direction, deadline, and resources.
- **Outputs**：Question card, nearest prior work, falsifiable predictions, and feasibility.
- **Acceptance**：Object, comparison, outcome, and counterevidence are clear; unknowns remain unknown.
- **Continue when**：The question is bounded and the next evidence check is specified.

## Literature and Evidence

- **Inputs**：Defined question, search boundary, and authorized literature.
- **Outputs**：Search log, comparison table, and claim-source-location ledger.
- **Acceptance**：Check citation identity separately from support; expose counterevidence and missing full text.
- **Continue when**：Key evidence is checked, or claims are narrowed enough to design validation.

## Data and Experiment Design

- **Inputs**：Claims, evidence ledger, data rights, baselines, and budget.
- **Outputs**：Split/evaluation protocol, claim-experiment matrix, and run order.
- **Acceptance**：Test data does not select methods; independent units and fair budgets are explicit.
- **Continue when**：A minimal check is authorized and its inputs/resources are ready.

## Reproduction and Runs

- **Inputs**：Agreed protocol, code version, lawful inputs, and run budget.
- **Outputs**：Separate run folders, raw results, logs, failures, and rerun commands.
- **Acceptance**：Status/counts come from actual logs; separate checks from formal runs.
- **Continue when**：Runs ended with traceable output; diagnose failure or hand off its gap first.

## Results and Figures

- **Inputs**：Raw outputs, protocol, evaluation version, and successes/failures.
- **Outputs**：Recalculable tables, figures, statistical notes, and claim evidence.
- **Acceptance**：Every number traces to a run; uncertainty, denominators, and limitations are explicit.
- **Continue when**：Claim scope is supported for writing; otherwise check further within budget.

## Manuscript and Citations

- **Inputs**：Actual methods/results, evidence table, citations, and writing requirements.
- **Outputs**：Manuscript, preview, citation/consistency checks, and author-statement gaps.
- **Acceptance**：No fabricated numbers/approval; prose, figures, tables, and citations agree.
- **Continue when**：Evidence/preview checks pass and target-venue preparation inputs are ready.

## Submission Preparation

- **Inputs**：Target venue, current official guidelines, final draft, and actual author details.
- **Outputs**：Local submission package, official-requirement map, and author confirmations.
- **Acceptance**：Actually open files; distinguish prepared from submitted.
- **Continue when**：After author confirmation, hand off to their authorized operator; this skill does not upload/publish.

## Revision and Response

- **Inputs**：Reviews of this manuscript, submitted version, deadline, and budget.
- **Outputs**：Original comments, revision matrix, new draft, evidence, and response draft.
- **Acceptance**：Each comment has a real response/location; unresolved issues are explicit.
- **Continue when**：With author confirmation and complete materials, hand off to the authorized submitter.

## Presentation and Discussion

- **Inputs**：Verified paper, audience, allotted time, and authorized materials.
- **Outputs**：Focused talk, slides, timed rehearsal, and question list.
- **Acceptance**：Evidence/figures match the manuscript and duration is actually rehearsed.
- **Continue when**：Use for the author-arranged presentation after confirmation; do not distribute independently.
